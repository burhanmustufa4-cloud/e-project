Our Expedition Services & Support

Comprehensive, professional support infrastructure tailored for international mountaineers and film crews across the Karakoram.

End-to-End Karakoram Logistics

At Alpine Ascents Pakistan, we provide seamless, professional support infrastructure tailored for international mountaineers, scientific researchers, and high-altitude film crews. From your arrival in Islamabad to your return from the Baltoro Glacier, every logistical detail is meticulously managed.

Permits, Visas & Government NOC

We handle all official paperwork with the Ministry of Tourism, Gilgit-Baltistan Council, and military liaison offices. This includes climbing permits, environmental fees, and satellite phone custom permissions.

Base Camp Management

Our base camps are designed for comfort and recovery. We provide spacious insulated dining tents, dedicated kitchen staff serving hygienic multi-course meals, solar charging stations, and satellite internet.

High-Altitude Porters & Crew

Work alongside the legendary Balti high-altitude porters and certified mountain guides. Our teams are equipped with top-tier insurance, professional climbing gear, and fair compensation.

Domestic Transport & Cargo

Reliable logistics from Islamabad to Skardu via secure road transport or mountain flights. We also manage porter weight distribution at Askole and secure freight forwarding for heavy gear.

Medical & Safety Infrastructure

Every expedition features comprehensive trauma kits, pulse oximeters, portable hyperbaric chambers (Gamow bags), and structured emergency evacuation protocols coordinated with military rescue.

Gear Rental & Equipment Support

Access high-quality expedition tents, fixed-line ropes, snow stakes, oxygen regulators, and composite high-altitude boots directly from our Islamabad and Skardu storage facilities.
